Brand Guide · V1

The Expert OS

How the brand looks, sounds and behaves. If you make anything that carries this name — a page, a post, a thumbnail, an email — it follows this document. Extend the system. Never redesign it.

01

Brand Story & Positioning

Imthiaz spent six figures on business, branding and content mentors. The best of it was locked behind £10K+ price tags and drip-fed over twelve months. Out of reach for the coaches who need it most.

The Expert OS is that knowledge condensed into one system. It was proven first in his own business, six figures in twelve months, and then in his clients'. TJ went from a collapsed gym business to strong monthly revenue in five months. Ash went from £1K to £8K months in the same window. Imogen went from zero to £12K launches.

The core idea: coaches don't have a business problem. They have a systems problem. The Expert OS is positioned as an operating system, not a course. That is why the entire visual identity borrows from premium hardware and software: the chip mark, the circuit grids, the Motherboard™.

The Villain

Overpriced, gatekept, drip-fed mentorship.

The Promise

Consistent monthly revenue through a system, not hustle.

Voice

Direct. Short sentences. Plain British English. Specific numbers over vague claims. No rhetorical flourish, no em-dashes, no "it's not X, it's Y" constructions. Sounds like a real person, not a template.

Rules (verified against Motherboard)

Orbitron is always uppercase, always tracked out (+0.1em via .heading-display), and never used for paragraphs.
Inter carries everything else: body copy, labels, buttons, data, tags — all roles the draft assigned to both Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono.
Labels use Inter at 10–12px, font-bold (700) or font-semibold (600), uppercase, tracked between 0.1em (tracking-widest) and 0.3em (tracking-[0.3em]).
Heading sizes in the Motherboard: text-xl (20px) to text-2xl (24px) for page titles. No clamp function used — Tailwind utility classes only.

Unverified — Landing Page / Marketing

The draft specified Space Grotesk (body) and JetBrains Mono (labels/data). Neither font is loaded or used anywhere in the Motherboard codebase. No landing page or how-it-works page currently exists in the codebase or at marketing.imthiazghulam.com. If these fonts are intended for a future marketing site, they should be documented separately when that site is built.

Hairlines & Radii

All borders are 1px. Glass card radius is 12px (0.75rem). Solid cards and buttons use 4–8px (rounded / rounded-lg). The chip mark frame uses 3px. No thick strokes, no pills, no full rounding. The hairline discipline is what makes the system feel like precision hardware — break it anywhere and the whole page cheapens.

07

Imagery & Thumbnails

Everything visual reads like a premium operating system booting up in the dark. Portraits are lit, not filtered.

■

Void-dark backgrounds on everything. No white, no colour fields.

■

Portraits get warm gold rim lighting on one side, bright enough to separate the subject from the dark.

■

Circuit-grid and chip motifs as texture, brighter near the subject, fading to the edges.

■

Text on thumbnails: Orbitron style, off-white with faint gold glow, large enough to read in a feed grid.

■

Flat and crisp. No 3D extrusion, no bevels, no chrome, no perspective tilt.

■

No stock-photo feel, no colours outside the palette, no more than one named mechanism per image.
